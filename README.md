# ChickenMart - Avin

ChickenMart is private business software built for the internal operation of the ChickenMart business. It records chicken purchases, supplier credit, customer credit, payments, expenses and Passbook activity.

## Ownership and use

This project and its source code are proprietary business property. They are stored here only to maintain the software and record development progress.

No permission is granted to copy, reuse, modify, distribute, sell, publish or deploy any part of this project outside the business without prior written authorization from the owner.

## Current progress

- Chicken receipt entry and purchase records
- Supplier credit and payment tracking
- Customer credit sales and collections
- Passbook with cash, UPI, credit and expense entries
- Customer credit statement printing
- Admin overview of available money and outstanding balances
- PostgreSQL database migrations, seed data and accounting tests

This is a private, no-authentication MVP and must not be publicly deployed with real business or financial data.
